About Islington Partners

A consulting and product studio focused on helping accounting firms and professional service businesses build more efficient, scalable operations.

What Islington Partners is

Islington Partners was created to bring together consulting, software implementation, automation, AI adoption, and product development under one coherent brand.

The business sits at the intersection of operations consulting and product development — working directly with firms to improve the way work gets done, and building practical software and training products based on the problems we see repeatedly in that work.

Our work is grounded in hands-on experience with real firms. We have worked with accounting firms across the UK, US, EU, and Canada, helping teams improve workflows, implement systems, train staff, and make better use of modern software.

Consulting

Hands-on workflow, systems, Karbon, AI, and automation consulting for accounting firms and professional service businesses.

Software products

Software tools built around specific operational problems in scheduling, client communication, and workflow management.

Training products

Structured training products helping professional service teams build skills in cybersecurity, systems adoption, and operational practice.

What we believe

The best firms will not scale by adding more complexity. They will scale by building better systems.

We believe the future of professional services will be shaped by firms that use software deliberately, automate repetitive work, train their teams properly, and apply AI where it creates real leverage.

These firms will have clearer workflows, better client data, tighter communication, and more scalable ways of delivering work. They will not be dependent on manual effort to hold things together.

Islington Partners exists to help firms move in that direction — through practical consulting, operational support, and software tools designed specifically for how these businesses work.

Who we work with

We work primarily with accounting firms and professional service businesses — firms that manage recurring client work, employ teams, and rely on software systems to deliver consistent output.

Our consulting clients are typically owner-managed firms, growing practices, or operations teams within larger firms who need focused support on a specific problem.

We work across the UK, US, EU, and Canada.

Primary focus areas

  • —Accounting firms (owner-managed and growing practices)
  • —Advisory and outsourced finance businesses
  • —Firms using or implementing Karbon
  • —Firms exploring AI, automation, and workflow improvement
  • —Professional service businesses with operational bottlenecks
  • —Firms preparing to scale, restructure, or modernise operations
