Projects

Products and experiments built around modern service businesses.

Products grounded in real problems

Our consulting work puts us inside accounting firms and professional service businesses. We see the same operational problems come up repeatedly — in scheduling, client communication, team training, cybersecurity, workflow management, and more.

Where we see a problem that is widespread, practical to solve, and not well addressed by existing tools, we build something.

The products we build are designed to work alongside consulting — not replace it. They are focused, specific, and built to be useful from day one.

How consulting and products connect

The products we build come directly from what we see in consulting. When a problem comes up again and again — in firm after firm, across different markets — it becomes a candidate for a product.

This means our products are not built on assumptions. They are built on direct, repeated exposure to real operational problems in the businesses we work with.

And our consulting is stronger because of the products. We understand the full software landscape for professional service firms — not just the major platforms, but the gaps between them.
