Cookies Notice

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

This Cookies Notice explains how Islington Partners LTD (“we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at islingtonpartners.com (the “Site”). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve your experience, and to provide information to the site operator. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels, which we refer to collectively as “cookies” in this notice.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Site functionality — to enable core features and keep the Site working correctly
  • User experience — to remember your preferences and settings
  • Analytics — to understand how the Site is used so we can improve it
  • Advertising measurement — to measure the effectiveness of our marketing

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Site to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They enable core features such as page navigation and security.

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personal features, such as your preferences and interface settings.

3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously or in aggregate. We use Google Analytics for this purpose.

3.4 Advertising Cookies

These cookies help us measure the effectiveness of our marketing and understand how visitors arrive at the Site. We use the Meta Pixel for this purpose.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on or integrate with the Site. These include:

  • Google (Google Analytics) — usage measurement and performance reporting
  • Meta / Facebook (Meta Pixel) — advertising measurement and audience insights

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which govern how they use cookies.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually block, delete, or be warned about cookies. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Site from working correctly.

For more information on managing cookies, please refer to the help documentation for your specific browser.

6. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Cookies Notice to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
