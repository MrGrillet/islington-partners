Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Islington Partners LTD (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website at islingtonpartners.com (the “Site”) or engage our consulting and product services.

1. Who We Are

Islington Partners LTD is a UK consulting and product studio. We help accounting firms and professional service businesses improve their operations through workflow design, software implementation, automation, AI adoption, and practical consulting, and we build and operate our own software and training products.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Company or firm name
  • Any details you include in enquiries, form submissions, or correspondence with us

2.2 Usage Data

  • IP address, browser type, and usage logs
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and actions taken

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the services you request
  • To communicate with you about projects and engagements
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our Site and services
  • To measure and understand how our Site is used
  • To ensure compliance with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data based on:

  • Contractual necessity (to provide the services you request)
  • Legitimate interests (to operate and improve our business and Site)
  • Consent (for marketing communications and non-essential cookies)
  • Legal obligations (e.g., financial and tax records)

5. Data Storage and Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Access controls and confidentiality obligations
  • Use of reputable service providers to store and process data

6. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with the following parties:

  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Advertising partners (e.g., Meta/Facebook)
  • Email and communication providers
  • Professional advisers and service providers under strict confidentiality agreements

We do not sell your data to third parties.

7. International Transfers

Your data may be transferred and stored outside your country of residence. We implement safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses) where required by law.

8. Retention Periods

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to respond to enquiries, provide our services, and comply with legal obligations. When data is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., UK GDPR, EU GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Access and export your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion (“Right to be Forgotten”)
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at office@islingtonpartners.com.

10. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for site functionality, user experience, analytics (e.g., Google Analytics), and advertising measurement (e.g., the Meta Pixel). See our Cookies Notice for full details.

11. Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. We will post any changes on this page with an updated effective date.
